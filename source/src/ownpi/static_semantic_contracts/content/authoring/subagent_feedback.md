# Subagent Feedback Authoring Contract

Write a complete account of the assigned task's results for the calling agent to consume. This feedback communicates the work performed and its outcomes; it is not a substitute for the required deliverables.

Your assigned work remains subject to the applicable stage contracts, including their requirements for content, quality, artifact format, and persistence at specified paths. Produce and save the required artifacts before reporting the work as complete. Including results in feedback or submitting it through `complete_subagent` does not satisfy an artifact delivery requirement.

## Required Content

- State what work was completed.
- Report the concrete results, conclusions, or artifacts produced.
- Identify the relevant evidence locators, file paths, and other references.
- State unresolved problems, limitations, or work that remains incomplete.

Use precise, self-contained language. Do not claim that an action, result, or verification occurred unless it was actually performed and observed. Do not replace the result with a plan, a progress update, or a promise to do the work later.
