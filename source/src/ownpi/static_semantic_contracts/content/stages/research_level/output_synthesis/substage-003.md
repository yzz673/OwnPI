## Completion Gate

Consider this block incomplete if any of the following requirements is unmet:

* Base the manuscript on the paper plan in `<workspace>/output_synthesis/paper_architecture_and_evidence_plan/`. Minor adjustments are permitted during drafting, but the manuscript must remain consistent with the plan.
* Complete every section, compile the main-text PDF, and place each figure and table in its intended location. Resolve typos, missing content, missing figures or tables, citation errors, compilation errors, and other basic defects.
* Check every section of the final manuscript directly against the Universal Writing Principles in `academic-paper-writing` and both documents of the selected profile, not from memory or prior acceptance alone. Record requirement-by-requirement results in the manuscript QA records and resolve every unmet applicable requirement.
* Maintain consistency and a coherent logical progression throughout the manuscript.
* Read the selected profile's example paper under the skill's `benchmark/` directory and compare writing quality: narrative coherence, clarity, concision, and presentation. Resolve obvious quality gaps; do not copy its content or impose its research scope, section lengths, or page allocation.
